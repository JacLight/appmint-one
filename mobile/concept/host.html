Questions

Hosting FAQ

What can I host?

Anything you can safely hold for a while — luggage and bags, event gear, bikes, coats, or a few parking bays. You choose which, set a price per item (or per hour), and set your capacity.

How does the handoff work?

The guest arrives with a code. You scan it, photograph the items and confirm the count — that's the drop‑off. On pickup, the code and photo must match before you hand anything back. It takes seconds and it's the same for every booking.

What if something goes wrong?

Every booking is covered by Stowbo protection up to $5,000. Because each handoff has a photo and count on record, claims are provable rather than he‑said‑she‑said. Prohibited and high‑value items are excluded — the full policy is shown at checkout.

How and when do I get paid?

Stowbo handles payments and settlement automatically. Your earnings (minus our fee) are paid out to your bank weekly via Stripe. No cash handling.

Is there a fee to list?

Listing is free. Stowbo takes a service fee (typically 15–20%) only when you actually earn — no bookings, no fee.
